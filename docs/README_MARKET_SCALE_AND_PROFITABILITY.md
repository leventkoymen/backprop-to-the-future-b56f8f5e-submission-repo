## 💼 Why We Are Strictly B2B (And Why It Bypasses Hospital Procurement)

Many healthtech startups fail because they attempt to sell clinical diagnostic software to hospital trusts, getting bogged down in 18–36 month clinical procurement cycles.

**LungPair is strictly a B2B Preclinical Software-as-a-Service (SaaS):**
1. **The Budget Source:** Sold directly out of academic and biopharma **departmental research consumable recharge accounts**.
2. **Sales Velocity:** 4 to 8-week procurement turnaround (director-level sign-off; no hospital trust board approvals needed).
3. **No Clinical Regulatory Drag Today:** Operating strictly as **Research Use Only (RUO)**. Zero FDA / MHRA clearance required for preclinical cell-line prioritization.
4. **Immediate ROI:** A single 50-pair screening campaign saves **$147,000 in physical consumable waste**—paying back the $35,000 annual software subscription in less than 4.2 months.
